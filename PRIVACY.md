# Privacy

The be-more-ambitious plugin contains only written instructions (a SKILL.md file). It has no scripts, makes no network calls, and does not collect, store, or transmit any data.

When you use it, the text you give Claude is handled by the Claude product you are using, under that product's own terms and privacy policy. This plugin adds nothing to that.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/Rebellion1991/be-more-ambitious/issues
